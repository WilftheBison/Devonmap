How to read this

Tribe sizes in each ward are estimates built from the 2021 Census (age, occupation, qualifications, housing), the rural-urban mix and how far each ward's estimated 2016 Leave vote ran above or below what its make-up predicts. Nobody counts tribes, so treat them as a way to compare strategies, not as a vote forecast.

A ward's score is the average appeal across its likely voters, from −2 to +3. The ranking ignores incumbents, candidates and Reform; those are for the group to weigh.

Change compares each ward's rank with the “Current offer” starting point.
